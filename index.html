二、中国 VS 香港 · ( WJP 法治数据对比 ) World Justice Project Rule of Law Index

法治指数是衡量国家或地区法治建设水平的量化评估体系，由“世界正义工程”（WJP）提出，旨在通过标准化指标评估法治实践成效。 The Rule of Law Index is a quantitative assessment framework that measures the level of rule of law in a country or region. It was proposed by the World Justice Project (WJP) and aims to assess the effectiveness of rule of law practices using standardized indicators.

该体系涵盖政府权力约束、廉洁程度、政府透明度、基本权利保障、秩序与安全、监管执法、民事司法、刑事司法等八个维度，结合调查统计与专家评审方式形成综合评分。 This system covers eight key dimensions: constraints on government powers, absence of corruption, open government, fundamental rights, order and security, regulatory enforcement, civil justice, and criminal justice. It combines survey statistics and expert reviews to generate an overall score.
